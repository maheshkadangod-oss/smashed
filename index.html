    Quick answers before you head over or order in.

    01Where is Smashed Burgers & Co. in Kochi?

    64/5257 G, Kathrikadavu, on Kaloor Kadavanthra Road in Kaloor, Kochi, Kerala.

    02What time does Smashed Burgers open and close?

    Every day from 6 PM to 4 AM, including weekends.

    03Where can I get a burger late at night in Kochi?

    Smashed Burgers & Co. in Kaloor is open until 4 AM every night, and delivers through Zomato during those hours.

    04Does Smashed Burgers deliver?

    Yes. Order through Zomato, where the restaurant is listed as Smashed Burgers & Co, Kaloor.

    05What is a smash burger?

    A ball of beef pressed flat onto a very hot flat-top griddle. The press gives the patty a thin, crisp, dark crust with lacy edges, which a thick grilled patty can't get.

    06What is the Animal Smash?

    Two smashed beef patties with signature animal sauce, double melted cheese and crisp lettuce. It's the house take on the California double animal style burger.

    07What are tallow fries?

    Fat-cut fries cooked in beef tallow instead of vegetable oil, then salted straight out of the fryer. They can also be ordered loaded with cheese sauce and smashed beef.

    08Is there anything other than beef?

    Yes. The Nashville Fried Chicken burger has crispy fried chicken, Nashville hot spice, pickles and house sauce.

    09How much does a meal cost?

    Zomato lists the average cost at about ₹500 for two people. Check the Zomato menu for current prices.
